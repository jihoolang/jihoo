# 0.6 release notes (planned)

0.6 is a feature, library and stabilization release. The original broad proof
milestone is deferred to future research, not declared complete. These notes
record scope; they do not change the version or announce a tag or publication.

## Implemented features

- [Dependent source ergonomics](../language/dependent-functions.md),
  [indexed records](../language/dependent-records.md) and module/privacy checks,
  with stabilization of dependent motives, annotations and proof-irrelevant
  conversion. Restricted local functions do not imply unrestricted closures.
- [Natural-number proofs](../language/natural-proofs.md) and
  [list proofs](../language/list-proofs.md): imported structural lemmas provide
  checked bounds and length evidence to executable consumers.
- [Sized lists](../language/sized-lists.md),
  [list-to-buffer transfer](../language/list-buffer-transfer.md),
  [buffer ranges](../language/buffer-ranges.md),
  [bounded collections](../language/bounded-collection.md) and
  [vector extension](../language/vector-extension.md). These use explicit
  dependent evidence; pointwise content preservation is not a general theorem.
- [Bounded proof automation](../language/proof-automation.md) builds ordinary
  checked Eq/Lt terms from local facts and explicit hints. Search remains
  incomplete and resource-limited; it does not add general automatic induction.

The linked language pages specify supported forms and restrictions. Regression
suites cover accepted and rejected evidence, exact erasure comparisons, native
behavior and selected both-target object checks. Such tests are implementation
evidence, not metatheorems or proof of every platform/profile combination.

## Mechanized results and trust boundaries

[Lean proof documentation](lean-proofs.md) and the
[proof-status assessment](0.6-proof-status.md) give the precise hypotheses.
The results include restricted pure dependent typing/conversion/substitution,
checked exported graph artifacts, quantity traces and heap/lease invariants;
restricted symbolic Buffer and fixed binary Tree ownership judgments;
compaction and execution correspondence for supported abstract runtimes; and
constructed Lean MIR lowering plus parametric proofs of selected native MIR
snapshots. These are different scoped results, not a single whole-language
accepted-program guarantee.

[Symbolic MIR totality](../../proofs/SYMBOLIC_MIR_STRUCTURAL_BRIDGE.md) retains
closing/inventory, ABI, width, layout and resource premises. The common Data
Case work includes conditional composition and restricted register safety;
entry, placement, finish and child-consumption results do not establish a
general CommonData-to-native compiler theorem. See the
[Case integration boundary](../../proofs/COMMON_CASE_INTEGRATION.md) and
[child-consumption connection](../../proofs/CASE_CONSUMPTION_INTEGRATION.md).
The latter constructs a restricted child BodyResult and a closed source/target
execution conjunction, not a general simulation or equal source/target traces.

The native checker is tested, not proved correct. Exporter/source association
where used, production lowering correspondence, MIR-to-LLVM, LLVM, and concrete
runtime/allocator behavior remain trust boundaries or open proof obligations.
Abstract successful allocation does not prove physical allocator success.
Certificate validity establishes only the statement checked for its artifact
and premises. This release does not claim full compiler verification.

## Preserved future goals

The [roadmap](roadmap.md) retains the original goals: imported induction lemmas
used to prove a loop's ordinary `ensures`, broader theory libraries, common
specified-Core typing-to-runtime ownership and borrow safety, full required
pure preservation/consistency and erasure coverage, and general lowering
correspondence. Typed loop postconditions and imported induction consumers
already exist, but ordinary `requires`/`ensures` syntax in marked dependent
modules and that original completion criterion are not thereby implemented.

No percentage of full soundness is inferred from tests, theorem counts or PRs.
Release-candidate validation and publication remain separate from this scope
change; consult the actual checks for the candidate revision before release.
